# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance & Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard analyzing historical pizza sales performance, customer ordering trends, category revenue breakdown, and top/bottom selling menu items.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key KPIs

The dashboard provides actionable operational and revenue insights for pizza store operations:
- **Total Revenue:** Overall gross sales generated.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Mean spend per customer order.
- **Total Pizzas Sold:** Total volume of pizzas prepared and dispatched.
- **Total Orders:** Distinct customer transaction count.
- **Average Pizzas per Order:** Basket size metric measuring order volume per transaction.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Views

### 1. Sales Performance & Trend Overview
> Analyzes time-based ordering trends, sales proportions across pizza categories, and customer size preferences.

![Sales Performance Overview](https://github.com/sukhdevmiyatra/Power-Bi-Pizza-sales-DashBoard/assets/141604120/f6d8422b-fbca-47d6-95f2-6e35362d7895)

**Key Insights:**
- **Daily & Monthly Order Trends:** Identify peak operating days (e.g., weekends/Fridays) and busiest months to optimize kitchen prep and staff allocation.
- **Category Contribution:** Highlights revenue and volume contribution of Classic, Supreme, Chicken, and Veggie categories.
- **Size Distribution:** Evaluates sales proportion across sizes (Regular, Medium, Large, XL, XXL) to forecast raw material and inventory needs.

---

### 2. Best & Worst Sellers Analysis
> Ranks pizza menu performance across three critical metrics: Revenue, Quantity Sold, and Total Orders.

![Best and Worst Sellers Analysis](https://github.com/sukhdevmiyatra/Power-Bi-Pizza-sales-DashBoard/assets/141604120/947625ee-67a3-493d-a31c-a039cc479c57)

**Key Highlights:**
- **Top 5 Performers:** High-margin and high-volume stars to focus marketing campaigns and promotional bundles around.
- **Bottom 5 Underperformers:** Items requiring recipe adjustments, pricing realignment, or phasing out from the menu to reduce food waste.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated

- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & Analytics:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures and columns
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query for data cleansing, standardization, and schema modeling
- **Visualization Best Practices:** KPI cards, line charts, donut charts, bar charts, and interactive slicers/filters
